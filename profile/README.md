# Hornbook

### Local-first learning tools that keep the most sensitive data in the world exactly where it belongs: in the room.

> A hornbook was a child's first lesson — a single lettered page you could hold in one hand. This one is the same promise, kept with modern tools: learning that stays close, stays yours, and never has to leave home to be useful.

A classroom holds some of the most consent-heavy, privacy-sensitive data there is: children, their names, their struggles, their progress. Most education software answers that by shipping it all to someone else's servers and asking you to trust the terms of service. Hornbook answers it the other way. Our tools run on the teacher's own machine, keep student data local and deletable, and keep a human in the loop for every judgment that matters.

Nothing here phones home. Nothing here ranks a child behind their back. The record is yours to keep and yours to delete.

## What's inside

**[UTETY](https://github.com/hornbook-knowledge/UTETY)** — the University of Technical Entropy, Thank You. A playable, conversational university where you learn *by talking* — a faculty of characters who model warmth, curiosity, and the honest fallibility of the tools themselves. Learning here is low-stakes, unranked, and built around care rather than certainty. *Start at [utety.pages.dev](https://utety.pages.dev).*

**[Jeles](https://github.com/hornbook-knowledge/Jeles)** — a verified-corpus organ: human-checked question/answer pairs with citations, served as a lookup layer before live search. Also ships a standalone MCP server.

**[oakenscrolls-office](https://github.com/hornbook-knowledge/oakenscrolls-office)** — shared office and publishing surface for Hornbook projects.

## The promise we make to a classroom

- **Local-first.** Student data lives on your machine, in formats you can read and delete. No accounts required to think. No cloud dependency to teach.
- **Human-governed.** The software proposes; the educator decides. Consent is explicit and expires with the session — nothing is retained or shared on the tool's own authority.
- **Durable.** Your records don't rot, don't drift, and don't disappear when a vendor sunsets a product. What you keep, you keep.

**Tools, not traps.**
